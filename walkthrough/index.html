13 Days to Escape Walkthrough: Day 1 to Day 3 for Beginners

Unofficial guide. Checked in-game on 13 and 14 November 2026. Last updated 16 November 2026.

This walkthrough follows our own runs of 13 Days to Escape on Roblox. Every number and on-screen text here is from our screenshots. In the 14 November 2026 run we reached the morning of Day 3. For background on each system, see the main guide.

New Prison is coming

The New Prison is not out yet. An in-game event card, “New Prison Release Party,” lists the opening time as Saturday, 21 November 2026 at 2:00 PM ET / 11:00 AM PT / 19:00 UTC. The card says: “A new prison is about to open. Join us, explore it and plan your escape!” After it opens, we will play it ourselves and update this walkthrough with first-hand New Prison notes. The steps below are from the current prison.

Full-run overview

This is a high-level picture of a whole run. The Day 1 to Day 3 steps further down the page are from our own screenshots. Anything marked “reported by other players / not yet tested by me” comes from public guides, not from our runs. Where those guides disagree with our screenshots, we keep our own numbers and notes.

The goal

Act normal by day and work on your escape by night. The official game text says prisoners taken behind the STAFF ONLY door after lights out do not come back the same, so you want to get out before that happens to you. Other players report that you lose if you are still inside on Day 13. We have not reached Day 13, so we have not confirmed that limit ourselves.

The daily schedule

Every day follows the same periods (checked in-game): ROLL CALL → LIBRARY → LUNCH → YARD TIME → RECREATION TIME → RETURN TO CELLS → LOCKDOWN. Use the day for jobs and shopping. Use lockdown to unscrew the toilet and dig. Be back in your cell before the “CELL DOORS CLOSE IN …” countdown ends.

How to earn cash

Cash is what you spend at OJ’s Deals. From our screenshots: the library pays 13 per book, peeling potatoes pays 5 each (25 for all five), and 8 pieces of yard trash pay 25. Hits have paid 35 or 70. Some public guides list lower job pay than this; we keep the numbers from our own screen. Other players also report cash on a transport truck if you leave the cell through the window — we have not tried that.

Suspicion

The bar at the bottom is SUSPICION. The objective always says “Don’t get suspicious.” At 100% you are caught, and the game shows the reason. We have been caught for “Punched guard” and for “Moved toilet found in your assigned cell.” Cameras can put a red cone on you. A tip on screen says “Dig at night to avoid suspicion.” After you dig, always put the toilet back.

Escape routes

What we have done: buy a screwdriver and spoons, unscrew the toilet in your cell, move it aside, drop into the hole, and dig with a spoon. The objective becomes “Dig your way out.” Always put the toilet back before morning. We reached the morning of Day 3 this way, but we were caught because we left the toilet moved, so we have not finished the tunnel.

Reported by other players / not yet tested by me: a faster route is to buy a Saw as well, cut the cell window bars during lockdown, climb out, take a free Power Drill from a workshop, put the bars back, then dig the toilet tunnel with the drill. Those guides say the tunnel can lead out to sea cliffs and a boat, and that some players escape as early as Day 2. We have not photographed the workshop, the Saw in the shop list, or an ending screen, so treat that route as a plan to test later. Other players also report a second prison after the first escape; we have not reached that.

Items

OJ’s Deals (checked in-game) sells a Screwdriver (25), Spoon (15), Flashlight (30), Knife (60), Scrap trowel (80) and Bat (50). A Day 1 kit of 2 spoons and 1 screwdriver is 55 cash. The shop has a scrollbar, so there may be more items below. Other players report a Saw and a Power Drill further down the list; we have not scrolled there yet.

Classes

In the lobby we photographed Prisoner (free), Hustler (20 diamonds), Medic (30) and Boxer (40). The list scrolls, and Engineer appears in an Escape Pack for 50 diamonds. Other players describe more classes, such as Murderer and Spy. Those guides often skip Boxer, which we did see on the board, so we only treat a class as confirmed when we have a screenshot.

Guards, rituals and the red room

Guards and cameras are the main daytime threat. Guards also hold a ritual around a red pentagram with candles from time to time (we have photographed this, but we do not know what it does). In our run a skull marker appeared on Day 2 even though we had not killed anyone, and a blood trail led to the red door — a likely explanation is that a guard killed someone the night before, not a confirmed fact. Inmates talk about a “red room,” and we have seen a pale, deformed inmate; we still do not know where that room is. Other players treat the STAFF ONLY door as the story threat, not as a door you open to escape.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the toilet moved after digging (we were caught at 100% for this).
  • Punching a guard.
  • Being seen by a camera while doing something illegal.
  • Missing the return to cells countdown.
  • Reported by other players / not yet tested by me: leaving the window bars off after you saw them, in the same way as leaving the toilet moved.

Before you start

  • Press E to use things (jobs, books, doors, bodies). C crouches. Ctrl unlocks the mouse.
  • The top bar shows the day, the current period and a timer. The right side shows your Objective. The bottom shows your SUSPICION level.
  • You start with 0 cash. Your cash is shown in the bottom-left corner.
  • The rule that never changes: “Don’t get suspicious.” At 100% the screen shows “SUSPICION REACHED 100%” with the reason.

Day 1: earn money for a screwdriver and spoons

Day 1 starts with ROLL CALL (checked in-game). The objective on screen is: “Buy a screwdriver from OJ.”

From our own run: spend Day 1 doing jobs to earn cash. The most useful Day 1 buys were 2 spoons and 1 screwdriver, which costs 55 cash at OJ’s Deals (15 + 15 + 25). The screwdriver is for unscrewing the toilet. The spoons are for digging the tunnel. A Scrap trowel costs 80 cash and, according to the shop, digs twice as much soil — a pricier upgrade if you have extra money. Other items did not feel useful yet in this run.

  1. LIBRARY. Go to the guard at the library desk. His bubble shows a book with a number (ours was #095) and the pay: 13 cash. Find the book with the same colour and number on the shelves, press E (“Take”), bring it back and press E (“Deliver book”).
  2. LUNCH. Go to the kitchen station and press E (“Peel potatoes”). Peel all 5 potatoes: 5 cash each, 25 in total. After one set, the cook said “Back tomorrow!”, so do it once per day.
  3. Hit (optional, risky). At lunch an inmate may offer a hit: a red KILL card with a target, a reward and a timer of about 4 minutes. We have seen a 35 cash inmate hit and, on another Day 1 lunch, a 70 cash hit whose portrait looked like a guard. Press E (“Accept hit”) only if you are ready. Check for cameras first, and drag the body away afterwards (press E on “Body · Drag”).
  4. YARD TIME. Pick up cans and paper on the ground. When you have 8, hand them to the Janitor by the trash can (E, “Hand in trash”) for 25 cash.
  5. RECREATION TIME. A short free period (about 1 minute).
  6. Buy from OJ. Buy the screwdriver, and two spoons if you can, as soon as you have the cash. At OJ’s Deals a Screwdriver is 25, a Spoon is 15, and a Flashlight is 30 (70 together). The Day 1 kit of 2 spoons and 1 screwdriver is 55 cash. OJ also sells a Knife for 60, a Scrap trowel for 80 and a Bat for 50. The cell wall sells a Knife for 29 cash — a different seller, and a different price. We did not find the Knife useful this early.
  7. RETURN TO CELLS. The screen shows “CELL DOORS CLOSE IN …” (we have seen 14s and 8s). Go back to your cell. Once you own the screwdriver, the objective already changes to “Unscrew the toilet” — we saw this on Day 1 return, not only on Day 2.
  8. LOCKDOWN. If you already have the screwdriver, you can unscrew the toilet now (see Day 2). Without one, the toilet says “Screwdriver required.” When you are done, sleep at your bunk to skip the night (“SLEEP AT YOUR BUNK TO SKIP · 0/1 SLEEPING” when solo).

Day 2: unscrew the toilet and start digging

Objective on screen (after you own the screwdriver): “Unscrew the toilet.”

  1. ROLL CALL. Day 2 starts here. Check your hotbar for the Screwdriver and Spoons.
  2. LIBRARY and LUNCH. Do the same jobs again for more cash (the library still paid 13 on Day 2).
  3. Hits. Hits can appear again. Be careful with guards: punching a guard took our suspicion straight to 100% (“Punched guard”). Don’t try this without a weapon.
  4. Night: unscrew the toilet. The toilet in your cell has 4 screws (“Screw 1/4 · Unscrew”). Use the screwdriver on each one.
  5. The hole. After the screws are out, the toilet moves aside and a hole opens in the floor. The objective changes to “Dig your way out.” The game also shows a tip: “Dig at night to avoid suspicion.”
  6. Dig with a spoon. Drop into the hole and dig with the spoon. The spoon has a durability number: it started at 240/240 for us, and was down to 108/240 after we dug on Night 2. That is why two spoons felt useful in this run.
  7. Put the toilet back. When you finish digging for the night, always put the toilet back in place. If you leave it moved, you will get caught in the morning.

Day 3 morning: hide the traces or you get caught

From our own run on 14 November 2026: we reached the morning of Day 3, but we had not put the toilet back after digging. Suspicion went straight to 100%. The screen said “SUSPICION REACHED 100%” with the reason “Moved toilet found in your assigned cell.”

Things we noticed (from our own run)

  • The skull marker is not only from a player kill. In this run it appeared on Day 2 even though we had killed no one. A likely explanation — not a confirmed fact — is that a guard killed someone on the night of Day 1. On Day 2 we found a blood trail in the hallway leading to the red door.
  • Guards sometimes hold a ritual. We have now photographed this more than once: several guards stand around a red pentagram with white candles. We saw it in the cafeteria at Day 1 lunch, and again in the cell block at Day 2 roll call. We still do not know what the ritual does.

After Day 3 (not confirmed yet)

We have not got past this catch on the morning of Day 3, so we have not finished the tunnel ourselves. Public videos show a Power Drill on a workshop bench and a Saw used on the cell window bars. We have not reached those steps with our own screenshots, so treat them as a plan, not a promise.

Common ways to fail

  • Leaving the toilet moved after digging: suspicion goes to 100% (“Moved toilet found in your assigned cell”).
  • Punching a guard: suspicion goes to 100% at once.
  • Being seen by a camera (red cone) while doing something bad.
  • Leaving a body in the open — it can still be there the next day.
  • Not being back in your cell before the cell doors close.
