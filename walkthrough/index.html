13 Days to Escape Walkthrough: Day 1 and Day 2 for Beginners

Unofficial guide. Checked in-game on 13 November 2026. Last updated 13 November 2026.

This walkthrough follows our own runs of 13 Days to Escape on Roblox. Every number and on-screen text here is from our screenshots. Steps we have not reached yet are marked clearly. For background on each system, see the main guide.

Before you start

  • Press E to use things (jobs, books, doors, bodies). C crouches. Ctrl unlocks the mouse.
  • The top bar shows the day, the current period and a timer. The right side shows your Objective. The bottom shows your SUSPICION level.
  • You start with 0 cash. Your cash is shown in the bottom-left corner.
  • The rule that never changes: “Don’t get suspicious.” At 100% the screen shows “SUSPICION REACHED 100%” with the reason.

Day 1: earn money for the screwdriver

Objective on screen: “Buy a screwdriver from OJ.”

  1. LIBRARY. Go to the guard at the library desk. His bubble shows a book with a number (ours was #095) and the pay: 13 cash. Find the book with the same colour and number on the shelves, press E (“Take”), bring it back and press E (“Deliver book”).
  2. LUNCH. Go to the kitchen station and press E (“Peel potatoes”). Peel all 5 potatoes: 5 cash each, 25 in total. After one set, the cook said “Back tomorrow!”, so do it once per day.
  3. Hit (optional, risky). At lunch an inmate may offer a hit: a red KILL card with a target, a reward of 35 cash and a timer of about 4 minutes. Press E (“Accept hit”) only if you are ready. Check for cameras first, and drag the body away afterwards (press E on “Body · Drag”).
  4. YARD TIME. Pick up cans and paper on the ground. When you have 8, hand them to the Janitor by the trash can (E, “Hand in trash”) for 25 cash.
  5. RECREATION TIME. A short free period (about 1 minute). Our Day 1 hit target went down during this period.
  6. Buy from OJ. Buy the screwdriver from OJ’s Deals as soon as you have the cash. We ended up with a Screwdriver, Spoon and Flashlight for 70 cash in total. (We have not confirmed each price yet.)
  7. RETURN TO CELLS. The screen shows “CELL DOORS CLOSE IN 14s”. Drop everything and go back to your cell.
  8. LOCKDOWN. If you already have the screwdriver, you can try the toilet now (see Day 2). Without one, the toilet says “Screwdriver required.” When you are done, sleep at your bunk to skip the night (“SLEEP AT YOUR BUNK TO SKIP · 0/1 SLEEPING” when solo).

Day 2: unscrew the toilet

Objective on screen (after you own the screwdriver): “Unscrew the toilet.”

  1. ROLL CALL. Day 2 starts here. Check your hotbar: Screwdriver, Spoon and Flashlight if you bought them.
  2. LIBRARY and LUNCH. Do the same jobs again for more cash (the library still paid 13 on Day 2).
  3. Day 2 hit. Our Day 2 hit was on a guard, for 70 cash. Be careful: punching a guard took our suspicion straight to 100% (“Punched guard”). Don’t try this without a weapon.
  4. Night: the toilet. The toilet in your cell has 4 screws (“Screw 1/4 · Unscrew”). Use the screwdriver on each one.

After Day 2 (not confirmed yet)

We have not got past Day 2 yet. Public videos show the route continuing like this: move the toilet aside, drop into the tunnel, and dig with a Power Drill found on a workshop bench, with a Saw used on the cell window bars. We will update this page with our own screenshots once we reach those steps.

Common ways to fail

  • Punching a guard: suspicion goes to 100% at once.
  • Being seen by a camera (red cone) while doing something bad.
  • Leaving a body in the open — it can still be there the next day.
  • Not being back in your cell before the cell doors close.
