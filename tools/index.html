Tools and OJ’s Deals

Prices checked in-game on 14 November 2026. The longer guide is on the main page.

From our own run, the most useful Day 1 buys were 2 spoons and 1 screwdriver, which costs 55 cash (15 + 15 + 25). A Scrap trowel costs 80 cash and, according to the shop, digs twice as much soil — a pricier digging upgrade.

The shop window is titled OJ’s Deals and also shows “BLOCK / B”. It has a scrollbar, so there may be more items below the six we photographed.

Item Price Shop text / notes
Screwdriver 25 Unscrew toilet bolts
Spoon 15 Dig beneath your cell. A new spoon starts at 240/240 durability.
Flashlight 30 Light up dark tunnels. We have not used it in a dark tunnel yet.
Knife 60 at OJ’s Deals Stab at close range. The cell wall also sells a Knife for 29 cash — two different sellers, two prices.
Scrap trowel 80 Dig twice as much soil. We have not used one yet.
Bat 50 Heavy melee weapon. We have not used one yet.

Other players report more shop items further down the list, such as a Saw and a Power Drill. We have not scrolled there yet, so we will not treat those prices as confirmed.
