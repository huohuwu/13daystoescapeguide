13 Days to Escape Guide (Unofficial Walkthrough)

For the Roblox game 13 Days to Escape [ALPHA] by RoDark Studios. Last updated 13 November 2026.

You wake up in a prison cell with no release date. By day you work prison jobs, earn cash, and try to look like an ordinary inmate. By night you work on your escape. The official description puts it simply: act normal by day, plan your escape by night.

This is a free unofficial guide. Facts marked “checked in-game” come from our own play session on 13 November 2026 (Day 1 and Day 2, with screenshots). Other details come from the official Roblox listing or public player reports, and we say so. Play 13 Days to Escape on Roblox — solo, or with up to three friends.

New here? Read the Day 1 and Day 2 beginner walkthrough.

What to do each day

Every day is a timetable. The bar at the top of the screen shows the day (for example DAY 01), the current period, and a countdown. When the timer runs out, the next period starts and the other inmates walk to the next area.

Checked in-game (13 November 2026): we saw all seven period names below. Day 1 went LIBRARY → LUNCH → YARD TIME → RECREATION TIME → RETURN TO CELLS → LOCKDOWN. Day 2 started with ROLL CALL, then LIBRARY and LUNCH. (We did not catch the very start of Day 1, so we can’t say if it also has a ROLL CALL.)

Period Length (public reports) Where / what to do
ROLL CALL Short Start of the day in the cell block. On Day 2 the objective changed here (see the escape section).
LIBRARY About 1 minute 30 seconds Library. Deliver the numbered book the desk guard asks for — 13 cash per book (checked in-game).
LUNCH About 2 minutes Cafeteria and kitchen. Peel potatoes for 5 cash each (checked in-game). Inmates may also offer you a hit here.
YARD TIME Not clearly timed Fenced yard. Pick up 8 pieces of trash and hand them to the Janitor for 25 cash (checked in-game).
RECREATION TIME About 1 minute A short free period, indoors and outdoors.
RETURN TO CELLS About 15 seconds Go back to your cell. The screen shows “CELL DOORS CLOSE IN 14s” counting down (checked in-game).
LOCKDOWN Night You are locked in. The screen says “SLEEP AT YOUR BUNK TO SKIP · 0/1 SLEEPING” — sleep in your bunk to skip the night (checked in-game, solo).

The timers we saw in our screenshots fit the lengths above, but we did not time every period from start to finish. ALPHA updates may change them.

Suspicion, guards and cameras

The objective panel always says “Don’t get suspicious.” A SUSPICION bar at the bottom of the screen shows your level from 0% to 100%. Here is what we saw in-game:

  • When a guard notices you, a ! (or !!) bubble appears over the guard’s head.
  • Security cameras hang in halls and rooms, including the library. When a camera sees you doing something bad, it shines a red cone at you. In our run, suspicion hit 97% while a camera cone was on us.
  • At 100% the screen shows “SUSPICION REACHED 100%” and the reason underneath. Ours said “Punched guard”.

How to make money

You start with 0 cash. Cash is what you spend at OJ’s Deals. Press E at a job to start it. All numbers below were checked in-game on 13 November 2026:

Job When What you do Pay
Library books LIBRARY The desk guard shows a book number (ours was #095). Find it on the shelves, press E to take it, then press E at the desk to deliver it. 13 cash per book
Peel potatoes LUNCH Kitchen station. Peel 5 potatoes (“Peel potatoes 2/5” and so on). Press Q to leave. 5 per potato, 25 for all 5. After one set the cook said “Back tomorrow!”, so it looks like one set per day.
Yard trash YARD TIME Pick up cans and paper on the ground. Bring 8 pieces to the Janitor by the trash can and press E (“Hand in trash”). 25 cash for 8 pieces
Hits (bounties) Any time you are offered one See below. Risky. 35 (Day 1, inmate target), 70 (Day 2, guard target)

Hits: how killing pays

Killing only pays because someone put a bounty on that person. In our run, an inmate offered a hit: a card with a red KILL label, the target’s picture, the reward, and a timer (about 4 minutes). Press E (“Accept hit”) to take it. The card then stays in the bottom-left corner until the timer ends.

  • Day 1 hit: an inmate, 35 cash. We got the 35 after the target went down (both times we tried).
  • Day 2 hit: a guard, 70 cash. Guards are dangerous — see the survival tips.
  • After the target goes down, press E (“Body · Drag”) to drag the body away.

A full Day 1 with one book, one set of potatoes, the yard trash and one hit is about 13 + 25 + 25 + 35 = 98 cash. In our second run we had 111 cash by the end of Day 1.

Beginner survival tips

These come from our own runs (we have not reached Day 3 yet — most punishments taught us something):

  1. Don’t fight guards without a weapon. Punching a guard sent our suspicion straight to 100% (“Punched guard”), and guards can fight back hard.
  2. Watch the cameras. Before you attack someone or move a body, look for a camera. If you see a red cone on you, get out of its view.
  3. Only kill for a hit. The money comes from the bounty, not from the kill itself. Accept the hit first.
  4. Hide the body. Drag it somewhere out of sight. In our run, a body we left in the open was still there the next day.
  5. Library trick: the book in the guard’s bubble has the same colour and number as the one on the shelf. Look for the colour first, then check the number.
  6. Yard trash: no trick — just pick up 8 pieces and hand them in.
  7. Potatoes: one player tip — keep turning the potato in one direction and hold the button. It felt a little faster for us, but not by much.
  8. Be in your cell on time. When “CELL DOORS CLOSE IN …” appears, stop what you are doing and go back.

The STAFF ONLY door and the 13 days

The official game description says that after lights out, prisoners are taken behind the STAFF ONLY door, and they do not come back the same. Your goal is to escape before it is your turn.

Checked in-game: there is a red door marked STAFF ONLY at the end of the hallway next to the library. In our Day 1 and Day 2 runs we did not see a timer or a “countdown prisoner” that shows who is taken next. We have not opened the door.

Escape: how it starts

Checked in-game (13 November 2026):

  1. Day 1 objective: “Buy a screwdriver from OJ.” Earn cash with jobs first.
  2. At night (LOCKDOWN), the toilet in your cell has 4 screws (“Screw 1/4 · Unscrew”). Without a screwdriver it says “Screwdriver required.” So buy the screwdriver before LOCKDOWN if you want to start on night 1.
  3. Once you own the screwdriver, the objective changes to “Unscrew the toilet” (we saw this at ROLL CALL on Day 2).

Not confirmed by us yet: public videos show the next steps as moving the toilet aside, going down into a tunnel and digging with a Power Drill, plus a Saw for the cell window bars. We have not reached these steps ourselves, so treat them as a plan, not a promise.
