You wake up in a prison cell with no release date. By day you work prison jobs, earn cash, and try to look like an ordinary inmate. By night you work on your escape. The official description puts it simply: act normal by day, plan your escape by night.

This is a free unofficial guide. Facts marked “checked in-game” come from our own play sessions on 13 and 14 November 2026 (through the morning of Day 3, with screenshots). Other details come from the official Roblox listing or public player reports, and we say so. Play 13 Days to Escape on Roblox — solo, or with up to three friends.

How a full run works

A short picture of the whole game. Details we have not confirmed ourselves are marked clearly. The longer outline is on the walkthrough.

  • Goal. Act normal by day, work on your escape by night, and get out before you are taken behind the STAFF ONLY door.
  • Each day. ROLL CALL → LIBRARY → LUNCH → YARD TIME → RECREATION TIME → RETURN TO CELLS → LOCKDOWN (checked in-game). Do jobs and shop by day; unscrew the toilet and dig at night. Be back before the cell doors close.
  • Cash. From our screenshots: library 13, five potatoes 25, 8 yard trash 25, optional hits 35 or 70. A Day 1 kit of 2 spoons and 1 screwdriver costs 55 cash.
  • Suspicion. Keep the bar down. We have been caught at 100% for punching a guard, and for leaving the toilet moved after digging.
  • Escape. We have unscrewed the toilet and dug with spoons. Always put the toilet back. Other players report a faster route with a Saw, a free workshop drill, and a boat at the end of the tunnel — we have not tested that yet.

What to do each day

Every day is a timetable. The bar at the top of the screen shows the day (for example DAY 01), the current period, and a countdown. When the timer runs out, the next period starts and the other inmates walk to the next area.

Checked in-game (13 and 14 November 2026): we saw all seven period names below. Day 1 starts with ROLL CALL, then goes LIBRARY → LUNCH → YARD TIME → RECREATION TIME → RETURN TO CELLS → LOCKDOWN. Day 2 followed the same pattern from ROLL CALL.

Period Length (public reports) Where / what to do
ROLL CALL Short Start of the day in the cell block. Confirmed on Day 1 and Day 2.
LIBRARY About 1 minute 30 seconds Library. Deliver the numbered book the desk guard asks for — 13 cash per book (checked in-game).
LUNCH About 2 minutes Cafeteria and kitchen. Peel potatoes for 5 cash each (checked in-game). Inmates may also offer you a hit here.
YARD TIME Not clearly timed Fenced yard. Pick up 8 pieces of trash and hand them to the Janitor for 25 cash (checked in-game).
RECREATION TIME About 1 minute A short free period, indoors and outdoors.
RETURN TO CELLS About 15 seconds Go back to your cell. The screen shows “CELL DOORS CLOSE IN …” counting down (we have seen 14s and 8s).
LOCKDOWN Night You are locked in. Work on the toilet and the tunnel, then sleep in your bunk to skip the rest of the night. The screen says “SLEEP AT YOUR BUNK TO SKIP · 0/1 SLEEPING” when solo (checked in-game).

The timers we saw in our screenshots fit the lengths above, but we did not time every period from start to finish. ALPHA updates may change them.

Suspicion, guards and cameras

The objective panel always says “Don’t get suspicious.” A SUSPICION bar at the bottom of the screen shows your level from 0% to 100%. Here is what we saw in-game:

  • When a guard notices you, a ! (or !!) bubble appears over the guard’s head.
  • Security cameras hang in halls and rooms, including the library. When a camera sees you doing something bad, it shines a red cone at you. In our run, suspicion hit 97% while a camera cone was on us.
  • At 100% the screen shows “SUSPICION REACHED 100%” and the reason underneath. We have been caught for “Punched guard” and, on the morning of Day 3, for “Moved toilet found in your assigned cell.”

How to make money

You start with 0 cash. Cash is what you spend at OJ’s Deals. Press E at a job to start it. All numbers below were checked in-game on 13 and 14 November 2026:

Job When What you do Pay
Library books LIBRARY The desk guard shows a book number (ours was #095). Find it on the shelves, press E to take it, then press E at the desk to deliver it. 13 cash per book
Peel potatoes LUNCH Kitchen station. Peel 5 potatoes (“Peel potatoes 2/5” and so on). Press Q to leave. 5 per potato, 25 for all 5. After one set the cook said “Back tomorrow!”, so it looks like one set per day.
Yard trash YARD TIME Pick up cans and paper on the ground. Bring 8 pieces to the Janitor by the trash can and press E (“Hand in trash”). 25 cash for 8 pieces
Hits (bounties) Any time you are offered one See below. Risky. We have seen 35 (inmate target) and 70 (the portrait looked like a guard)

Hits: how killing pays

Killing only pays because someone put a bounty on that person. In our run, an inmate offered a hit: a card with a red KILL label, the target’s picture, the reward, and a timer (about 4 minutes). Press E (“Accept hit”) to take it. The card then stays in the bottom-left corner until the timer ends.

  • We have seen a 35 cash hit on an inmate, and a 70 cash hit whose portrait looked like a guard. Both appeared during lunch in our runs, including on Day 1.
  • Guards are dangerous — see the survival tips.
  • After the target goes down, press E (“Body · Drag”) to drag the body away.

A full Day 1 with one book, one set of potatoes and the yard trash is 63 cash before any hit. In our second run we had 111 cash by the end of Day 1.

Beginner survival tips

These come from our own runs, including the 14 November 2026 run that reached the morning of Day 3:

  1. On Day 1, do the jobs and buy 2 spoons and 1 screwdriver (55 cash). From our own run, those were the most useful early buys. The screwdriver unscrews the toilet; the spoons dig the tunnel. A Scrap trowel (80) is a pricier digging upgrade. Other items did not feel useful yet.
  2. After you dig, always put the toilet back. If you leave it moved, you get caught in the morning at 100% suspicion. The reason on screen was “Moved toilet found in your assigned cell.”
  3. Don’t fight guards without a weapon. Punching a guard sent our suspicion straight to 100% (“Punched guard”), and guards can fight back hard.
  4. Watch the cameras. Before you attack someone or move a body, look for a camera. If you see a red cone on you, get out of its view.
  5. Only kill for a hit. The money comes from the bounty, not from the kill itself. Accept the hit first.
  6. Hide the body. Drag it somewhere out of sight. In our run, a body we left in the open was still there the next day.
  7. Library trick: the book in the guard’s bubble has the same colour and number as the one on the shelf. Look for the colour first, then check the number.
  8. Yard trash: no trick — just pick up 8 pieces and hand them in.
  9. Potatoes: one player tip — keep turning the potato in one direction and hold the button. It felt a little faster for us, but not by much.
  10. Be in your cell on time. When “CELL DOORS CLOSE IN …” appears, stop what you are doing and go back.

Tools and OJ’s Deals

OJ is the inmate who runs the shop OJ’s Deals (the shop window also shows “BLOCK / B”). On Day 1 the objective panel says “Buy a screwdriver from OJ” (checked in-game). We saw OJ standing in the cell block at Day 1 roll call, with a cash bubble over his head.

From our own run: the most useful Day 1 buys were 2 spoons and 1 screwdriver, which comes to 55 cash (15 + 15 + 25). The screwdriver unscrews the toilet. The spoons dig the tunnel and wear down as you dig (ours went from 240/240 to 108/240 on Night 2). A Scrap trowel costs 80 cash; the shop text says it digs twice as much soil, so it is a pricier digging upgrade.

Prices below are from our OJ’s Deals screenshot on 14 November 2026. The shop has a scrollbar, so there may be more items below the six we photographed.

The STAFF ONLY door and the 13 days

The official game description says that after lights out, prisoners are taken behind the STAFF ONLY door, and they do not come back the same. Your goal is to escape before it is your turn.

Checked in-game: there is a red door marked STAFF ONLY at the end of the hallway next to the library. We have not opened the door, and we still have not seen a timer or a “countdown prisoner” that shows who is taken next.

From our own run on Day 2: a blood trail in that hallway led to the red door, with scratch marks and a pool of blood at the base. We had not killed anyone in that run. A likely explanation is that a guard killed someone on the night of Day 1 — this is our observation, not a confirmed fact. A skull marker also appeared on Day 2 even though we had not killed anyone; it does not only appear after a player kill.

Escape: how it starts

Checked in-game (13 and 14 November 2026):

  1. Day 1 objective: “Buy a screwdriver from OJ.” Earn cash with jobs first. From our own run, the most useful early buys were 2 spoons and 1 screwdriver (55 cash).
  2. At night (LOCKDOWN), the toilet in your cell has 4 screws (“Screw 1/4 · Unscrew”). Without a screwdriver it says “Screwdriver required.” So buy the screwdriver before LOCKDOWN if you want to start on night 1. Once you own it, the objective changes to “Unscrew the toilet” (we saw this already on Day 1 return to cells).
  3. After the screws are out, the toilet moves aside and a hole opens in the floor. The objective becomes “Dig your way out.” A tip on screen says: “Dig at night to avoid suspicion.”
  4. Drop into the hole and dig with a spoon. The spoon wears down as you dig (ours went from 240/240 to 108/240 on Night 2).
  5. Always put the toilet back before morning. If you leave it moved, suspicion hits 100% with the reason “Moved toilet found in your assigned cell.” That is how our Day 3 morning ended.

Not confirmed by us yet: public videos show a Power Drill on a workshop bench and a Saw for the cell window bars. We have not reached those steps ourselves, so treat them as a plan, not a promise.

  • The “red room”. An inmate in the yard said: “Nobody talks about the red room.” Earlier we also heard: “They dragged him into the red room. He came back deformed.” We have seen a pale, deformed inmate (number 247363 in our screenshot). We don’t know where the red room is.
  • An electric chair scene. Once, right after our suspicion hit 97%, a cutscene showed guards strapping a prisoner into an electric chair. We are still testing what triggers it.
  • Staying awake at night. One of us stayed up instead of sleeping and heard scary sounds, but didn’t see what it was.
  • The Flashlight. OJ’s shop text says “Light up dark tunnels.” We have not used it in a dark tunnel yet.
  • Cell 13 and the workshop. We have not photographed these yet.

What happens if I leave the toilet moved?

You get caught in the morning. Suspicion goes to 100% with the reason “Moved toilet found in your assigned cell” (checked in-game on our Day 3 morning).

Yes. The official description says you can play solo or team up with up to three friends. When playing alone, the night screen shows “0/1 SLEEPING”, so you can skip the night by sleeping in your bunk.
